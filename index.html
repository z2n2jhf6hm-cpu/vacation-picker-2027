How it works

1 · About youName, how many protected weeks you get (12, 11, or Steve 6), and your A/B/C split.
2 · Tap weeksChoose a mode (Protected / Extra / Avoid), then tap weeks on the calendar. Tap a picked week again to change it.
3 · Put them in orderIn “Your lists”, drag or use ▲▼ so #1 is the week you want most. Mark SET or FLEX, and ★ up to 3 must-haves.
4 · Send itTick the consent box, press Download CSV, and email the file back. That's it.

SET = only this exact week works (booked trip, wedding). FLEX = the week before or after is fine too. Weeks run Sunday–Saturday. Your answers save automatically in this browser on this device — nothing is uploaded anywhere and no login is needed.

1About you

Protected weeks (A/B/C) are your 11 or 12 guaranteed, non-recallable weeks — matched first. Track your own split: never more than your split of each, and nobody takes more than 4 A weeks. Using priority to win a conflict moves you to the bottom of the priority list.
Jordan / Trevor: your guaranteed weeks are set aside before matching; for 2027 they can count as A or B (group to confirm) — mark them as Protected and pick A or B. Steve: 6 protected weeks (2A/2B/2C), January–June only.

2Pick your weeks

Choose what a tap does, then tap any week. Tap a picked week to change its type, seat (A/B/C), SET/FLEX, must-have, or note.

Tap adds as:
Protected (A/B/C) · P1 = rank Extra / buy later · X1 = rank Avoid (can't / won't be off) ★ Must-have 12 holiday ⚠ Extra seat unlikely (still selectable)

3Your lists — put them in order

#1 = the week you want most. Drag (computer) or use ▲▼ (phone). If you get 11 protected weeks, list a 12th (or more) as backups.

Extra weeks to buy later

Picked in a separate round after all protected weeks are set. 44 for sale, no limit on how many you list; they can be recalled if staffing requires and never become protected.

Weeks to avoid

Up to 5 — weeks you can't or don't want off (e.g. you've agreed to work).

4Extras & your 12th week

Extras sell one pick per round until all 44 are gone.
The three 11-week partners take one extra week as their 12th before the open extra round.
An extra week is given back only after the hospital has cut staffing needs. Leftover partial-week days are clustered when feasible (e.g. Thu–Fri); unwanted days go to the group, then become V-prime days. No extra-week seat in the 5 July weeks after Steve retires or 3 of the August weeks (Trevor's orientation — which 3 is TBD).
